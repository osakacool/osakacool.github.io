Note 1: Missing Value Structure (MNAR). The "Raw Missing Rate" reflects the inherent nature of destructive metabolomics sampling and Limit of Detection (LOD) in LC-MS/MS, which constitutes Missing Not At Random (MNAR) data. Traditional linear interpolation fails here.
Note 2: Derivation to 0% Missing. As detailed in Section 4.3, Eq. 17, all missing values in the derived tensors are imputed using a metabolite-specific Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) conditioned on concurrent growth traits and historical hidden states, followed by Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) with circadian kernels for temporal smoothing. Thus, the "Derived Missing Rate" is strictly 0.0%.
Note 3: Causal Normalization. All features subsequently undergo causal Exponential Moving Average (EMA) dynamic normalization (Eq. 18-19) to ensure no future information leakage during training or inference, strictly adhering to the real-time forecasting requirement.
Note 4: Unit Standardization. Metabolite concentrations are uniformly converted to μmol/g FW (Fresh Weight) or ng/g FW for hormones to ensure scale invariance prior to neural operator input.
